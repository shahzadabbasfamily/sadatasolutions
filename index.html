Who we are

A software company incorporated in Pakistan, built for the world.

SA Data Solutions (SMC-Private) Limited designs, develops, and maintains modern digital solutions for businesses worldwide. Our expertise spans custom software development, enterprise applications, AI-powered automation, cloud technologies, web platforms, and mobile applications.

We help organizations streamline operations, improve efficiency, and accelerate digital transformation through applied technology — built and supported by an in-house development team.

As part of our international service portfolio, we provide software development and technical support to overseas clients, including FutureDBuild Ltd (United Kingdom).
